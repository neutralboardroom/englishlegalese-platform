# Roger's Durable Rule — RDR-002
## No dilution, no loss of intention; additions only when genuinely useful

**Rule ID:** RDR-002  
**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED · ACTIVE · ADDITIVE · PERMANENT  
**Owner directive confirmed:** 2026-11-21  
**Scope:** Every present and future Roger-owned project, website, application, agent, workstream, builder, automation, Universal Next-Version Builder, governance register, integration and release process; includes independent non-Teacher-Roger projects.  
**Relationship:** This is an **additional safeguard**. It does **not** replace, amend, supersede, renumber, narrow or relax RDR-001, the Maximum Useful Improvement principle, any existing Roger's Durable Rule, any project-specific safety/ownership/release standard, or any prior owner intention.

### Binding owner instruction

> **Never weaken, silently narrow, remove or disregard existing Roger's Durable Rules, approved intentions, safeguards, ownership boundaries, working features, or established protections merely to make a change or add a new rule. Add or change something only when it creates a genuine, explainable benefit, and preserve all earlier valid requirements. When in doubt, retain the stronger existing requirement and stop the affected change until the owner resolves any genuine conflict.**

### Operational requirements

1. **Cumulative inheritance by default.** Every new rule, feature, design, source version, Builder, integration, migration and process improvement inherits **all applicable older active requirements** and their intent, not just convenient excerpts. Maintain the exact source text, identifier, version/date, authoritative location and provenance. An abbreviated summary is not a replacement for the original rule.
2. **No dilution through reinterpretation.** Do not soften an absolute safeguard into a recommendation; silently convert a required test/approval into an optional check; drop a difficult obligation; treat absence of evidence as passing; change a rule's scope to suit a release; transfer ownership between independent builders; or claim a mock, prototype or plan is deployed functionality. A newly written rule cannot grant permissions prohibited by existing rules.
3. **Benefit-before-change test.** State the actual problem, risk, missed opportunity or learner/customer/owner benefit; identify what will materially improve and how that can be verified. Prefer repairs and necessary compatibility/security/accessibility/quality upgrades over cosmetic churn or artificial version inflation. **No change is a valid successful decision** if the current implementation works well and the proposal provides no material improvement.
4. **Preserve working value.** Protect approved appearance, information architecture, accessibility, curricula and original educational quality, business rules, offline reliability, privacy, data, interfaces, controls, integrations, tests, deployment paths, previous versions and full usable rollback materials. Test important existing behavior as well as the proposed benefit; revert or hold a regression rather than conceal it.
5. **Document tradeoffs.** Before a consequential change, record the inherited obligations, expected benefit, evidence, affected functions, testing, cost and complexity, risk to prior intentions, rollback, and deferred alternatives. If a new requirement appears to conflict with an old one, **pause the conflicting part** and seek an explicit owner decision. Never infer that a newer timestamp alone revokes an older rule.
6. **Only the owner can deliberately amend owner intent.** An explicit later owner instruction may intentionally modify or withdraw a specific prior requirement, but that amendment must identify the exact affected rule/scope, reason, authorization date, preservation/rollback implications, and the specific text changed. Keep the original record intact and create an append-only, versioned successor/cross-reference. The rest of the rule corpus remains fully in force. This RDR-002 does not nullify a genuine explicit owner amendment.
7. **Cross-project boundaries and higher protections remain.** A shared directive is not permission for a builder to edit a sibling's source, appropriate its assets or students, activate payment/PII/voice/avatar providers, merge a release, deploy, or fabricate the independent recipient's approval. Child protection, consent, professional correctness, privacy, medical/legal compliance, release gates, source lineage and backups stay cumulative.
8. **Durable enforcement in future versions.** Every owning builder should include the exact RDR-002 text and ID in its authoritative cumulative Builder and rule register at its next authorized build, with an explicit **NO_RULE_DILUTION / MEANINGFUL_BENEFIT / NO_REGRESSION** check. ControlCenter should record dissemination and independent adoption evidence separately. **A published document is not proof every builder has read it.**
9. **Continuous improvement without pointless modification.** Continue identifying opportunities across relevant facets—teaching, correctness, reliability, mobile/desktop use, performance, accessibility, cost, security, privacy, backup/recovery and operating workflows—but make only justified, verified, authorized changes. Do not keep changing working software just to appear productive.

### Minimum audit evidence for each meaningful change

A release/handoff record should identify: (a) newest separate complete source and cumulative Builder, (b) the active rule corpus and predecessor lineage, (c) why this change is worthwhile, (d) what prior capabilities must not regress, (e) evidence of tests and approvals, (f) a backup/rollback path, (g) any unverified or blocked requirements, and (h) genuine external acknowledgments where applicable. A zero-change decision should briefly say why preserving the current behavior best serves the owner.

### Adoption and non-overwrite notice

This rule is effective as an **owner-issued rule** on 2026-11-21, and is published for TR-ControlCenter and all relevant independent builders to reconcile at their next authorized checkpoint. **Independent receiver acknowledgments are pending** unless separately evidenced. Publishing it does not by itself mutate an existing app, universal Builder, source release, registry, or automation, nor does it authorize a production deployment. Keep every older rule and recovery artifact intact.
