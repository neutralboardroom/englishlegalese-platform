4. Respond when the facts change

The fictional client or colleague has supplied new information. Your first confirmation may need revision. Read the changed facts before deciding; this is not a new live message.

My own revised-answer checklist (not an automatic grade):
  • Did I acknowledge what actually changed?
  • Did I preserve what remains unconfirmed?
  • Did I identify the correct person, action and conditional timing?
  • Did I avoid promising a result not supported by the update?
