**Scope:** Legal-English specialists, assessment products, AI speaking tutors, teacher marketplaces and corporate language-training platforms.  
**Method:** Current public/official product material reviewed; patterns are adopted/adapted only, not copied verbatim or as proprietary content.

## v1.55.0 concrete adoptions
1. Legal Scenario Studio with 21 original/synthetic professional scenarios across seven practice categories.
2. Three-level practice progression: Foundation / Professional / Advanced.
3. Personalized four-week learning path based on goal, level, weekly time and priority skills.
4. Legal Skills Passport separating completed practice from learner self-checks.
5. Mistake/review loop through one saved next-focus note per practice.
6. SmartTeacher AI scenario handoff with legal-education boundary.
7. Learner-controlled Teacher Review Packet.
8. Privacy-safe employer progress snapshot that excludes drafts, raw AI conversations and confidential facts.

Under EL-RGR-014, every future material release must refresh this research rather than treating this ledger as permanently current. New competitor capabilities, evidence, standards and user expectations must be reassessed and explicitly dispositioned.
