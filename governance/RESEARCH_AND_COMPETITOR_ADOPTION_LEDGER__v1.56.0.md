# EnglishLegalese v1.56.0 — Fresh Research + Competitor Adoption Ledger

**Research date:** 2026-10-17  
**Rule:** EL-RGR-014  
**Scope:** fresh current research for this material release; prior research is not treated as a substitute.

## Current sources reviewed
- EF Corporate Learning 2026 language-training maturity research — AI + personalization increasingly differentiate higher-maturity programs; organizations want measurable program impact.
- EF Corporate Learning AI language-learning product pages — guided AI assistants, roleplay, instant feedback, personalized remediation, human-teacher augmentation, live progress tracking.
- Preply Business Corporate Dashboard (2026 help documentation) — attendance/activity, onboarding progress, goals, satisfaction and manager reporting in one place.
- Existing EnglishLegalese legal-English competitor set retained for comparison: TOLES, TransLegal, lawyer-led Legal English schools, AI tutors and teacher marketplaces.

## Decisions for v1.56.0

### ADAPT — Progressive disclosure instead of giant landing-page catalogs
Competitors increasingly separate acquisition/overview pages from deep practice environments. EnglishLegalese adapts this by showing featured courses/scenarios on the homepage and keeping the complete existing libraries available through explicit expand controls. This reduces cognitive load without deleting content or routes.

### ADAPT — Guided AI entry points
AI language products use contextual starters rather than leaving learners with an empty chatbot. EnglishLegalese adds original guided SmartTeacher starters for client updates, contract-clause explanation, meeting clarification and AI-draft review while preserving free-form input.

### ADAPT — Personalization as an intentional next action
The personalized four-week plan remains intact but no longer occupies full homepage height by default. The learner explicitly opens the planner when ready.

### ADAPT — Progress visibility with stronger truth boundaries
Corporate platforms emphasize visible progress. EnglishLegalese retains progress while making the initial state more useful and explicitly distinguishes learner practice/self-checks from accredited testing, certification and teacher-confirmed feedback.

### ADAPT — Employer visibility with learner privacy
Preply/EF-style manager visibility is useful, but EnglishLegalese narrows the pattern: aggregate/consent-based progress signals only; private drafts, raw AI work, recordings, uploads, native-language explanations, test answers and teacher-private notes are excluded by default.

### ADOPT — Human + AI complementarity
EnglishLegalese continues the pattern of AI practice plus human teacher review rather than presenting general-purpose AI as a replacement for teaching or professional judgment.

### REJECT_WITH_CAUSE — Fake or unsupported proficiency scoring
Some platforms can provide validated language assessments because they possess assessment infrastructure and evidence. EnglishLegalese v1.56.0 does not invent CEFR, TOLES, pronunciation, fluency or accredited proficiency scores. A starting diagnostic is described only as a learning/placement starting point unless later validation supports stronger claims.

### REJECT_WITH_CAUSE — Manipulative streaks / pressure mechanics
No streak pressure, fake mastery, scarcity or engagement mechanics are added merely because consumer language apps use them. Professional learning quality and truthful progress take priority.

### DEFER_WITH_REASON — Live speech analysis
Potentially valuable, but this release does not claim live pronunciation/speech scoring because qualified speech-analysis infrastructure and measurement validity have not been proven.

### DEFER_WITH_REASON — Enterprise SSO/SCIM and deep corporate analytics
Useful for mature enterprise customers, but not appropriate to broaden a homepage architecture release before authentication/authorization and active buyer requirements justify the complexity.

## Release implication
The v1.56.0 homepage changes are original EnglishLegalese implementations. No competitor branding, proprietary course content, protected expression, private data or scoring methodology is copied.
