**Authority:** Owner request of 18 November 2026: “dont forget to always check and read updates etc from the control panel” and explicit confirmation “yes that should be a durable no drift rule for you.” This rule is **ACTIVE and cumulative**, additive to original EnglishLegalese Roger Rules and `EL-RGR-020`; it does not transfer product ownership, authorize a deployment, or override existing privacy and release requirements.

## Binding operating rule

**MANDATORY BEFORE EVERY BUILD** of EnglishLegalese application, website, AI host adapter, or Universal Next-Version Builder: TR-EnglishLegalese MUST inspect and read the current TR-ControlCenter panel and project handoff documents **read-only** and record an evidence-bearing, timestamped pre-build currentness receipt. Never begin a new source version from assumptions about the last chat. The review must happen again when resuming a stopped build or after a newer ControlCenter artifact arrives. This is not optional even when a build appears unrelated to avatar integration.
