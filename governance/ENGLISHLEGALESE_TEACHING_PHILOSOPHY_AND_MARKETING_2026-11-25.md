## Owner-approved five-stage personalized teaching methodology
Owner approval: 2026-11-25. Apply to individual Legal English and Business English instruction and appropriate employer programs. This is an additive teaching and marketing standard, not a claim that a new automated system is already deployed.

1. **Understand the professional.** Establish the student's industry, role, actual tasks, audience, communication challenges, current English level, and personal learning objectives. Keep a task-based baseline rather than relying only on a grammar placement test.
2. **Select appropriate materials.** Use original realistic scenarios or authorized, minimized, carefully de-identified workplace documents. Confirm permission, scope, confidentiality and any applicable professional obligations before accepting materials. When in doubt, use fictional substitutes. Never invite real confidential documents into public practice tools.
3. **Build an individual learning plan.** Select workplace vocabulary, phrases, professional register, reading comprehension where relevant, and speaking tasks drawn from the student's real responsibilities. Integrate brief grammar explanations only where they support clarity or accurate communication.
4. **Practise real professional situations.** Rehearse realistic discussions, meetings, presentations, negotiations, client questions, contract explanations and correspondence appropriate to the learner's role. Model effective language, let the learner practise, give targeted feedback, then repeat with a meaningful variation. Teach language, not legal, financial or other regulated professional advice.
5. **Measure and adapt.** Compare comparable baseline and follow-up tasks using observed vocabulary accuracy, comprehension, clarity, fluency, appropriate register and task completion. Document examples of progress and next priorities; use teacher feedback and learner self-assessment where appropriate. Do not infer results without evidence or promise guaranteed improvement.

### Reusable private lesson-planning structure
Professional task and audience; learner goal; authorized material or fictional substitute; initial speaking sample; target vocabulary and expressions; realistic role-play; contextual correction; repeat task; observed performance and next-session priority.
