## Public wording
Our teaching philosophy prioritizes workplace vocabulary, speaking practice, appropriate professional communication, and measurable improvement over traditional grammar instruction. We teach grammar in context when it helps learners communicate clearly and accurately.

## Implementation
- Prioritize workplace vocabulary used in realistic professional contexts, spoken practice, and communication appropriate to audience, purpose and level.
- Use brief, context-specific grammar correction as needed; do not present grammar as irrelevant, eliminate writing/reading instruction, or promise grammar-free teaching.
- Define measurable improvement as progress against a baseline using repeatable task-based practice and teacher feedback where available: vocabulary accuracy in context, clarity, fluency and register, and ability to complete workplace communication tasks. Do not promise a numerical improvement, guaranteed fluency, accredited certification, or unimplemented progress tracking.
- Use this positioning consistently in the homepage teaching-method section, both course pages, future approved outreach and employer collateral. No automatic sending or alteration of prices, terms, qualifications, existing learning tools, approved homepage hero/image or role boundaries.
