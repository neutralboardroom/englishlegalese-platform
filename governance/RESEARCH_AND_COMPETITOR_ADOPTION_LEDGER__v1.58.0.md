# EnglishLegalese v1.58.0 — Research and Competitor Adoption Ledger

**Research date:** 2026-10-17  
**Method:** fresh ordinary web research; no Firecrawl credits used.  
**Scope:** Legal-English specialists, formal assessment/certification, AI language learning, and corporate language training.  
**Rule:** learn from patterns; do not copy protected expression, proprietary content, branding, or private data.

## Current evidence reviewed

### TransLegal
Current TransLegal course materials emphasize all four skills, teacher-led and self-study use, authentic legal documents, communicative case studies, and role-relevant Legal English. Its International Legal English materials span reading, writing, listening and speaking and use legal-professional scenarios.

**Disposition — ADAPT:** keep EnglishLegalese centered on integrated realistic professional tasks rather than isolated vocabulary drills. Preserve current Scenario Studio, four-skill framing, LL.M./career scenarios and teacher-review pathway.

### TOLES
TOLES currently offers Foundation, Higher and Advanced formal Legal English exams and issues certificates/scores. Its public materials distinguish exam levels and formal assessment from ordinary preparation/practice.

**Disposition — ADAPT:** preserve clear Foundation / Professional / Advanced practice progression, but keep the EnglishLegalese starting diagnostic explicitly non-accredited and non-equivalent to TOLES/CEFR/certification. Formal-assessment wording is rejected unless future validation and authorization support it.

### EF Corporate Learning / EFEKTA//26
EF currently emphasizes AI-native personalization, live teacher-led learning, role/industry relevance, live roleplay, structured journeys and measurable progress. Its 2026 materials also emphasize that AI should extend proven learning methods, retain human involvement, and connect learning activity to ability/outcomes.

**Disposition — ADAPT:** preserve SmartTeacher AI plus human/teacher guidance; keep guided starters and realistic roleplay; use progress signals carefully. Do not imitate EF proficiency/certification claims or speech measurement because EnglishLegalese does not currently have equivalent validated infrastructure.

### Preply Business
Preply Business currently emphasizes role/goal-specific personalized learning, 1-on-1 tutor support, AI practice between lessons, flexible scheduling, and manager visibility into progress.

**Disposition — ADAPT:** continue role/goal-based learning plans and teacher review; keep employer reporting aggregate/consent-based and private learner work excluded by default. Do not expose private drafts merely to make management reporting richer.

### ELSA Speak
ELSA currently emphasizes personalized AI learning paths, speaking/pronunciation technology and bilingual support that can help learners transition toward English.

**Disposition — DEFER_WITH_REASON:** bilingual/native-language bridge patterns fit EnglishLegalese, but speech/pronunciation scoring remains deferred until EnglishLegalese has truthful, validated speech infrastructure. Do not present text-guided conversation practice as live pronunciation analytics.

## Cross-competitor conclusions applied in v1.58.0

1. **ADOPT:** focused first action + progressive disclosure. The public homepage should not force every full catalog/scenario/progress detail into the initial scroll.
2. **ADAPT:** personalized starting guidance without false certification. Keep the diagnostic useful but explicitly non-accredited and non-persistent by default.
3. **ADAPT:** AI + human/teacher complementarity. SmartTeacher supports practice; teacher/human judgment remains distinct for nuanced/high-stakes interpretation.
4. **ADAPT:** realistic professional scenarios. Preserve all existing scenarios but feature a smaller initial subset.
5. **ADAPT:** employer visibility only at the appropriate level. Aggregate/consent-based progress may be useful; private learner work stays private by default.
6. **REJECT_WITH_CAUSE:** unvalidated claims of proficiency, certification, speech scoring, legal competence or AI accuracy. EnglishLegalese lacks the validated infrastructure to make those claims.
7. **REJECT_WITH_CAUSE:** homepage density as proof of product depth. A very long public page can reduce clarity even when the underlying functionality is valuable; functionality is preserved through progressive disclosure/deep links instead.
8. **DEFER_WITH_REASON:** enterprise SSO/SCIM, validated speech analytics, full cross-device learning persistence and formal employer analytics until authentication/privacy/retention foundations are production-qualified.

## Research truth boundary
Competitor certifications, validated assessments, learner-scale claims, corporate metrics and speech-analysis capabilities belong to those products. They are not evidence that EnglishLegalese has the same capabilities. v1.58.0 therefore preserves conservative public claims and explicit distinction between practice, learner self-check, AI feedback, teacher-confirmed feedback and formal accreditation.
