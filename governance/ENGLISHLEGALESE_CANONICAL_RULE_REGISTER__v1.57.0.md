## EL-RGR-001 — Product isolation / sister projects are read-only
EnglishLegalese is its own product and authority chain. Other Roger-owned projects may be studied read-only for transferable lessons, rules, release discipline, design/UX patterns, testing, safety and governance. Do not mutate, deploy, version or rewrite another project while building EnglishLegalese. Do not import another project's private data, profiles, contacts, credentials, regulated operating state, branding, public copy, pricing authority or product authority merely because a pattern is useful.

## EL-RGR-002 — Absolute Roger Rule permanence / zero rule drift
A Roger Rule remains active through every build, continuation, recovery, refactor, compaction, migration, deployment, rollback, handoff, successor, model change and autonomous improvement cycle until Roger explicitly changes or revokes it. A Roger Rule may not be omitted, weakened, silently reinterpreted, summarized away, renumbered away, replaced by builder preference, lost to context limits, treated as historical-only or silently superseded by automation, architecture or a builder-created rule. Any owner-authorized change must preserve original text/provenance and explicit predecessor/successor history.

## EL-RGR-003 — Universal product no-drift / no-regression
Future EnglishLegalese releases must have **no unexplained drift of any type**. Protected dimensions include product identity, rules, working features, routes, navigation, copy/claims, pricing/billing, visual brand/assets, accessibility, mobile behavior, SEO/metadata, privacy/data-purpose boundaries, AI safety, APIs/integrations, persistence/data contracts/migrations, tests, release lineage and deployment/runtime state. Every material change must be intentional, attributable, evidence-backed, tested where reasonably possible and reversible through a known recovery path. Unexpected deletion, lost feature, lost route, lost rule, broken integration, unexplained data-contract change, unexplained visual/UX change or unexplained state change is a RELEASE BLOCKER. On drift: do not seal; restore/rebase from the authoritative predecessor; narrow the change; retest.

## EL-RGR-004 — Adopt / adapt / reject only with cause
Every potentially transferable Roger Rule or durable builder rule discovered in EnglishLegalese or another Roger-owned project receives exactly one disposition: `ADOPTED`, `ADAPTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `REJECTED_WITH_CAUSE`. Silence is not a disposition. Transfer the lesson, not another project's authority or data.

## EL-RGR-005 — True newest authoritative predecessor first
Before every material release, identify the true newest authoritative EnglishLegalese predecessor using strongest available evidence: repository state, exact artifacts, manifests, release/deployment receipts, hashes and owner-approved recovery facts. Do not assume the numerically highest version, newest upload, old continuation note or timed-out chat is authoritative. If predecessor truth is materially uncertain, fail closed rather than silently fork lineage.

## EL-RGR-006 — Maximum reasonable safe material work
A material EnglishLegalese version should complete the maximum reasonable package of safe, useful, high-value, in-scope work that can be coherently completed and tested. Do not create thin version bumps, artificial fragmentation or token additions merely to advance version numbers.

## EL-RGR-007 — Preserve winners / no changes for the sake of changes
Preserve useful working functionality, content, routes, controls, discoverability, navigation, data and successful design patterns. Do not break, remove, obscure, bury, demote or make working appropriate functionality harder to find merely to create a new version. Intentional replacement must preserve or improve the underlying user benefit and have a documented reason.

## EL-RGR-008 — Public user-first language / no internal builder language
Public pages are for learners, teachers, employers and buyers—not builders. Do not expose unnecessary internal/developer terminology, readiness gates, deployment language, hidden rule names, implementation-state jargon, internal counts or test language on public surfaces.

## EL-RGR-009 — Privacy and data minimization
Collect, persist, share and expose only data reasonably necessary for learning, teaching, employer training, account, payment, support or operations. Do not invite confidential client facts into AI practice. Learner drafts, raw AI conversations, uploaded documents, private notes and confidential facts must not automatically become employer reports, marketing data, public content or unrelated analytics. Prefer minimal-data/local-device handling when durable server persistence is not necessary.

## EL-RGR-010 — Truthful state / no fabricated proof
Never fabricate or overstate accreditation, certification, CEFR level, legal qualification, teacher verification, completion, learner skill, AI accuracy, employer ROI, customer count, partner status, testimonial, result, deployment state, payment state, integration state or live availability. Practice completion, learner self-check, teacher-confirmed feedback, formal assessment and accredited certification are distinct states and must remain distinct.

## EL-RGR-011 — AI is a learning aid, not legal advice or silent final authority
SmartTeacher AI is for Legal English education and professional communication practice. It must not present itself as a law firm, lawyer, legal-advice service, final legal reviewer, certified translator or court interpreter. It may help users practice, structure, clarify and review language; actual legal meaning, strategy, filings and confidential matters require appropriate qualified human/legal review. Fluent language must not hide uncertainty.

## EL-RGR-012 — Mobile, accessibility and keyboard parity
Every material public-facing release must preserve or improve meaningful mobile usability, responsive layout, keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced-motion behavior and practical touch targets where relevant. Desktop success does not excuse a broken mobile or keyboard experience.

## EL-RGR-013 — Whole-product self-improvement every material version
Every material EnglishLegalese version must inspect the whole product, not only the newest feature. At minimum review: homepage/value proposition, onboarding, courses/content, SmartTeacher AI, scenario practice, teacher flow, employer flow, dashboards, pricing/billing if active, navigation, design consistency, mobile, accessibility, language support, SEO, performance, privacy/security, support/error/empty states, analytics/measurement, retention and release truth. Identify the largest real user/business problem, research better solutions, implement the strongest safe improvement, test it, preserve winners and name the next-best action. Do not wait for Roger to identify ordinary product improvements.

# Durable Builder Rules

## EL-DUR-001 — Canonical rule custody
Maintain stable IDs, full rule text, source/reason/date/scope, active state, predecessor/successor links and tests/evidence for all EnglishLegalese durable rules. Historical rule evidence remains immutable.

## EL-DUR-002 — Rule inheritance gate / fail closed
Before material product mutation and again before seal, prove zero missing Roger Rules, zero unauthorized Roger Rule changes, zero conflicting duplicate IDs, zero active rule-metadata contradictions, zero stale-authority conflicts and zero cross-project authority imports. If the rule corpus cannot be proven, stop the affected build/action rather than guess.

## EL-DUR-003 — Rule-to-test traceability
Every mechanically testable rule must map to tests, assertions, receipts or qualification checks. Non-executable governance rules require an explicit evaluated release assertion. Rule words in an archive are not sufficient; rule effect must be preserved.

## EL-DUR-004 — Builder rule successorship is transactional
A builder rule may be added for evidence-based reasons. It may be amended only with explicit successor linkage, or retired only when no higher rule depends on it or an equal-or-stronger successor is proven. Rule IDs are never reused. A builder rule never becomes a Roger Rule unless Roger explicitly makes it one.

## EL-DUR-005 — No loss through compaction, migration or recovery
Context reduction, smaller packaging, refactors, repository changes, new chats, renamed files, generated prompts, storage optimization, rollback and recovery may never remove current rule authority, provenance, required functionality or exact references needed for continuation.

## EL-DUR-006 — Current-state metadata must agree
Active current-state materials must not contradict one another about current product version, rule state, deployment/live state, pricing or public availability. Historical truth may remain historical but must not masquerade as current truth.

## EL-DUR-007 — Successor self-bootstrap without chat memory
A future EnglishLegalese builder must be able to recover the accepted authority chain from repository/release artifacts without relying on hidden memory, an old open chat or someone's recollection.

## EL-DUR-008 — Before/after no-drift evidence
Every material release must record predecessor identity, intentional change list, new artifacts/components, deleted/removed behavior list (normally NONE unless intentionally authorized), regression evidence, rule disposition, recovery evidence and explanation for every material behavior change.

## EL-DUR-009 — No false success from counts alone
Matching counts do not prove integrity. Rule checks must compare stable IDs, text/authorized successor, provenance, scope and active state. Product checks must verify behavior, not merely file presence.

## EL-DUR-010 — Safe engineering improvement remains allowed
No-drift does not freeze obsolete code. Implementation may change when affected Roger Rules remain satisfied, affected builder rules are preserved or explicitly succeeded, useful functionality/data/provenance are not lost, tests prove equal-or-stronger protection and recovery remains possible.
