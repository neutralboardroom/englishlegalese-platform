## EL-DUR-013 / EL-RGR-025 — Essential transactional email is permitted; unsolicited outreach remains separately controlled

1. **Permitted application transactional email:** EnglishLegalese may implement and, once the provider/domain, security, templates and real workflow are verified, send narrowly scoped, event-triggered operational email for **student account/email verification, password resets and recovery, login/security notices, authentic class or appointment confirmations and changes, material service notices, and teacher feedback/lesson notifications that are actually due to the affected enrolled learner**. A bona fide payment receipt may be sent only after a genuine confirmed payment and separate payment-system authorization. Transactional mail must arise from a genuine user action or validated event and go only to the correct intended recipient. Relevant user preferences must be respected. **This permission does not itself establish that any provider is connected or that a message was delivered.**
2. **Unsolicited outreach and marketing:** Website builds, CI, deploys and student-account actions **must not send, launch, queue, schedule or activate unsolicited bulk email, prospecting, recruiting, law-firm outreach, employer/school applications, promotional campaigns or mailing-list blasts**. Those need a **distinct explicit owner authorization**, an independently governed workflow, proper recipient permission where applicable, unsubscribe/opt-out handling, suppression/deduplication, and applicable legal compliance. Existing lead/contact research does not constitute sending approval. A generic “continue”, “build the next version” or “deploy” is not marketing authorization.
3. **Deployment/build safety:** The builder, its AI helpers, unit/browser tests, automated qualification jobs, migration scripts and release commands must **never send real emails or fire genuine email-triggering endpoints during testing or deployment**. Test behavior with fakes/stubs that have no outbound network delivery. A production application may send approved transactional messages **only when its real user event occurs and the sender is fully configured**. Shipping dormant transaction-mail code is not proof of activation.
4. **Technical gates before first real delivery:** Verify the domain and sender (SPF/DKIM and provider where applicable), transactional category and approved template, HTTPS/secret isolation, rate limits and abuse prevention, provider error/idempotency handling, privacy-safe logs, destination validation, and operational rollback/disable switch. Verification/password-reset tokens must be cryptographically random, time-limited, single-use and stored hashed; account recovery must avoid user enumeration. Do not claim an email was sent or a class confirmed until the provider/event explicitly confirms it. Never include passwords, confidential client materials, private homework or session tokens in general emails. Keep AI-trial verification limits and privacy gates separate.
5. **Student experience:** Students should create and log into accounts to save lessons durably. The interface distinguishes **server-confirmed “Saved to your account”** from voluntary **“Saved on this device only”**. Where transactional verification/reset is unavailable, state that accurately rather than claiming verified accounts, working email recovery, or delivered notices. Do not force students into an unverified-account-only future architecture in order to satisfy the outdated blanket email ban.
6. **Preserve existing projects and data:** Do not modify other projects' contact databases/outreach systems, connect a different project's email identity without approval, overwrite past verified notices, delete prior email governance evidence, or change the approved Neon production schema except through separately approved safe migrations. Preserve existing user accounts and their work.

## Mandatory future builder and acceptance protocol
- Every subsequent EnglishLegalese builder prompt/ZIP handoff, specialized builder, fork, release checklist and governance reading must load **EL-DUR-013 / EL-RGR-025** as the **current policy** and cite this file. Include it in no-drift and source gates; preserve the historical former text with a conspicuous supersession notice rather than silently rewriting evidence.
- Automated tests must positively check **approved transactional categories** and negatively check **unauthorized outreach, unverified sender claims and actual network sends from builds/tests**. A failure or inability to verify is **BLOCKED/UNKNOWN**, not a pass.
- Do not interpret this owner authorization as a request to immediately send a test email or promotional message. Delivery setup and live validation require separate operational checks, and any new external paid service or charges must be surfaced before activation.

**Historical scope clarification:** EL-DUR-011 and EL-RGR-023 remain mandatory protections against sending from builder processes and against unauthorized outreach.
