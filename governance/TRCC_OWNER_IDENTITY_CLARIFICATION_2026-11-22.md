## Exact definitions (use consistently)

1. **Human Teacher Roger** — the actual human Roger Gillman **personally teaching** in a physical classroom or live online class. This is a **human-instructor teaching modality**, distinct from a generic teacher employed by a school and distinct from owner/admin capabilities.
2. **AI Teacher Roger** — the authorized **AI clone of Roger Gillman**: its independently approved teaching intelligence, synthetic voice/avatar, provider adapter and interaction services belong to **TR-CloneFusion**. Generic device TTS, scripted roleplay and mock avatars are **not** AI Teacher Roger, and must never be presented as authentic provider-connected AI.
3. **Owner/Admin** — a **separate administrative product role held by Roger Gillman**. It governs the platform, user-account authority, oversight, access reviews, billing controls, scheduling policy and safety permissions only when securely authenticated/authorized. It is not synonymous with a human teaching session and is never assigned to all teachers or AI agents. The same actual person can act as Human Teacher Roger or Owner/Admin, **but the roles, interfaces, permissions, audit, and reason for access remain distinct**.

## Security and learner-records application

- Learners can use substantial free content as guests, no account/card required. Saving lesson progress, homework, and scores requires a secure optional account after the relevant privacy/release gates.
- In R41 adult-fictional local evaluation, a learner's saved records may be accessible to **the learner and authenticated Owner/Admin** under explicit platform policy, with owner access audited. The learner may **separately grant/revoke scoped access** (progress, assignment state, assessments, teacher review) for parents, employers, and ordinary/classroom teachers, subject to independent guardian/child safeguards where relevant.
- **Human Teacher Roger as an instructor** does not mean every classroom teacher receives administrative visibility. His instructional tools use their own teaching context; any privileged Owner/Admin action requires the separately authenticated owner/admin authorization. A human-instructor label is not a bypass of consent or privacy rules.
- **AI Teacher Roger never receives Owner/Admin rights or broad private pupil records** merely because its brand/voice is 'Roger'. Provider sessions, learner data, microphone, retention, consent, cost caps, STOP, and credits require separately approved scoped interfaces.
- Public copy, navigation, dashboard labels, integration contracts, records policy, test fixtures, handoffs, and future cumulative Builders must reflect these distinctions. Do not silently convert generic `teacher` role into `owner` or vice versa.
