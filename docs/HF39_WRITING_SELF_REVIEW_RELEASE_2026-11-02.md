## Why / meaningful before-after
Before: 24 original Legal English and Business English practice lessons offered writing boxes, model answers, knowledge checks, spoken rehearsal and explicit private .txt backup. Learners had no lesson-specific scaffold for checking whether their OWN draft distinguished uncertain from verified facts or gave a clear actionable next step. The existing knowledge check evaluates another selected statement, not the draft.
After: each of 24 lessons has three original topic-specific writing-review checks plus a challenge question, an optional private self-reflection checklist, accessible progress text, and word/character budget feedback; edits/restore clear previously ticked checks. Not a grade, certification or AI review. Neither writing nor check state is sent or stored.
