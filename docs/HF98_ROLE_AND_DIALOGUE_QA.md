## Pedagogical path (one complete staged loop)

1. Choose one of the original Legal/Business stories; read two authentic fictional professional English turns.
2. Select the correct meaning. An incorrect answer does **not** grant passage; original comprehension and explanation remain authoritative.
3. Say a full English reply; continue to an entirely different situation; attempt your reply before showing the original transfer model.
4. After the original model becomes visible, reveal **a new stakeholder question** that tests how the learner adjusts to new information or uncertainty.
5. Say or write a complete, polite reply; optionally self-review subject+verb, meaning/conditions and a practical next step; optionally reveal a truthful source-authored example. The learner is NEVER graded or told a device voice or scripted activity is AI Teacher Roger.

All six authored second-round cases are bound to their original case title *and exact original transfer prompt*: contract comment deadline/extension; NDA public information scope; client written instruction format; draft-versus-final report; action-item responsibility/deadline; orders-versus-guaranteed-revenue. A case change, Stop, Restart, topic switch, turn change, or pagehide removes the optional draft from the page and hides the previous example. No persistent answers, data transfer, microphone, teacher access, automatic assessment, payment, or authentication change.
