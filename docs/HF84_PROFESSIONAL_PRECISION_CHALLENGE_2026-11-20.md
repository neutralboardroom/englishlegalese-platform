## What was built

A fully self-contained, fictional-adult EnglishLegalese Professional Precision Challenge now extends the HF83 three-part reply-builder preview. Each of twelve existing Legal and Business English dialogue exchanges has a professionally authored prompt contrasting two believable English replies with a deliberately selected more precise example. The selection varies between the first and second options (6 each). This is fixed example comparison; no real semantic reasoning, automatic grading or live AI teacher.

The learner chooses an answer and deliberately checks it. An incorrect selection displays a non-revealing prompt to try again; neither the correct text nor explanation leaks until a correct check or deliberate reveal. The English and localized native rationale for each turn come from the independently inherited HF82 authoring source. General guidance is offered in en-US, th-TH, vi-VN, zh-CN and ko-KR. These translations are *unreviewed educational drafts*, not professionally approved. A separate Try Again resets the selected answer while keeping the practice panel open. Changing dialogue, stage, track or locale, STOP and reset clear the ephemeral response.

No account, actual student, client or pupil data, browser storage, network request, microphone, voice cloning, avatar or real legal advice is involved. Teacher Roger's proposed avatar remains future host work with independent provider/likeness/privacy approvals; nothing here claims a genuine conversation.
