# EnglishLegalese — optional 16-week professional English learning pathway (HF85 reference)

**Teaching guide only, not a new price, subscription obligation, certificate promise, or enrollment term.** Available only for fictional adult Legal English and Business English training. Sessions and attendance must be arranged by the teacher and learner; there is no automatic booking or AI lesson fulfillment in this HF85 prototype.

An optional structured 16-week pathway supports continuity without coercive enrollment. Each week combines teacher-led instruction, an independently completed scripted roleplay, feedback on precise professional English and an agreed next-session objective. Learners may review or end their engagement under the actual agreed enrollment and cancellation terms; this document does not change them.

| Week | Main Legal English goal | Main Business English goal | Evidence of practice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Clarify roles and goals | Clarify roles and goals | Personal target and diagnostic conversation |
| 2 | Contract language: must, shall, may | Status reports with estimates | Two carefully distinguished phrases |
| 3 | Questions about obligations | Asking effective clarification questions | Three-turn scripted dialogue |
| 4 | Plain-language summary of a clause | Concise project summary | Teacher feedback and checkpoint |
| 5 | Dates, notices and deadlines | Milestone updates | Written follow-up with dates |
| 6 | Explain permission versus duty | Accept collaboration diplomatically | Rewritten response with clear action |
| 7 | Negotiate a revised deadline | Negotiate work priorities | Roleplay both response paths |
| 8 | Review ambiguity without legal guarantees | Communicate uncertainty responsibly | Midpoint conversation and growth review |
| 9 | Professional tone in formal letters | Respectful client meeting openings | Compare phrasing and audience |
| 10 | Clarifying complex terms | Requesting evidence before conclusions | Scenario questions and roleplay |
| 11 | Document agreement in a recap | Record action items and owners | Polished written recap |
| 12 | Respond within scope; refer legal advice | Escalate risks without blame | Teacher-reviewed communication |
| 13 | Read fictional contract correspondence | Read fictional project correspondence | Extract key points and limitations |
| 14 | Professional negotiation simulation | Client stakeholder meeting simulation | Three-turn branched conversation |
| 15 | Refine independently written replies | Refine independently written replies | Revision with feedback, no AI grades |
| 16 | Capstone communication practice | Capstone communication practice | Teacher-led reflection and next goals |

**Safeguards:** No student activity is automatically saved or scored by HF85. No CEFR accreditation, legal qualification, guaranteed outcome or professional assessment is implied. Teachers should obtain consent before independently collecting or retaining any learner work, use the existing approved EnglishLegalese workflows and avoid uploading confidential matters or minors' details into the prototype. Any separate graded assessment or certificate must follow the independently approved production process.
