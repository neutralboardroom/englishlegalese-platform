## Problem and meaningful learner benefit
HF96 offers an authored explanation of each of the 12 existing fictional-adult Legal/Business English speaker turns after a student mentally explains its meaning. Students who want to practise *written* English have no private editor for an original full-sentence response before self-comparison. HF97 adds an optional 600-character in-page editor and an explicit, **human-controlled three-question self-review**: complete sentence with subject/verb, fidelity to the speaker's uncertainty/conditions, and who/when/deadline. The student may revise, compare with the preserved HF96 sample, and clear it. **It is not automatically graded or a teacher-reviewed submission.**

The draft exists only as a textarea value inside the current rendered page. It is **not submitted, stored, copied, downloaded, authenticated, transmitted, or scored**. It is erased when changing a speaker turn, changing lesson, stopping, restarting, subject change, or leaving the page. Both screen-readable text and optional device speech remain available if the browser has no speech engine. No localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, fetch/XHR, mic, cloud endpoint, cookies or AI provider introduced. The browser's own device services are separate from the site's behavior; spellcheck disabled in this editor.
