## Pedagogical scope

The existing adult Legal English and Business English offline lessons, vocabulary explanations, two-choice drills, three-turn fictional professional conversations, and private writing studio all remain. HF83 adds an optional **three-part reply builder** to the writing studio. For each of the 12 scripted conversation exchanges, it offers three meaningful English phrase models: a courteous opening, the essential factual/linguistic point, and a prudent next step or appropriate limitation. Each phrase is concealed until deliberately revealed. The learner can assemble all three into an **editable draft**, rewrite it, and use the already existing checklist and deliberate model comparison. A nonempty draft cannot be overwritten without a separate second confirmation.

The interface retains English-first directions and draft explanatory copy in English, Thai, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese and Korean. **Translations and legal/business meaning still require independent native-speaker and professional subject review**. These are fixed fictional-adult teaching exemplars, **not AI**, speech, scoring, legal advice, a live Teacher Roger avatar or an employer/learner assessment. No user content is stored, transmitted or recorded. The visible in-tab draft is cleared on course/language/scenario/turn transition, stop, reset and reload; actual device acceptance remains outstanding.
