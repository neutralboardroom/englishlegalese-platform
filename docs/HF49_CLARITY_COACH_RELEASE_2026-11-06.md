## Problem, evidence, expected benefit
The existing 24 professional Legal English, Business English and Investor English lessons include writing review, private draft export, paired dialogues, optional general device read-aloud, 50-minute guides, 72 teacher follow-up questions, clarification, worksheets and four-week learning paths. They lack **subject-specific connected-speech drills** teaching students where to pause, which information to emphasize, how to check that a listener understood, and how to respond to a misunderstanding. Owner's direct teaching experience identifies sentence production, pronunciation and listener comprehension as critical even for students with adequate vocabulary. This is meaningful pedagogy, not visual novelty.

**After:** all 24 lessons receive unique, original fixed fictional professional model phrases, three thought groups, a designated key information word to stress, user-controlled stepwise reveal/reset, optional user-initiated device/browser voice at normal or slow pace with stop and honest unsupported-device fallback, a unique partner comprehension question and concealed cautious model answer, teacher cue and an entirely ephemeral three-point reflection checklist. All text remains accessible without audio. No microphone, uploads, scoring, claim of CEFR level, external certified pronunciation assessment or real-time AI coach.

The new module is inserted **inside the existing already-collapsed HF48 more-tools disclosure within the already-collapsed HF37 speaking panel**, so the initial 12-lesson page height does not grow; preserves the previously problematic tablet WebKit full-page screenshot guard, and all old interactions remain discoverable.

## Research adoption
- Council of Europe CEFR phonological competence: `https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/phonological-competence`. Current scales value intelligibility, articulation and prosody (stress, rhythm, intonation), not imitation of an idealized native accent. **ADOPT:** focus on meaningful stress, manageable thought groups and listener understanding. **REJECT:** claims of standardized assessment or eradication of accent.
- British Council Connected Speech Part 1: `https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/professional-development/teachers/knowing-subject/connected-speech-part-1`. **ADAPT:** contrast full/natural phrasing and pair discussion of meaning; original proprietary lesson text not copied.
- Owner's real classroom feedback: students need manageable spoken turns, exact teacher prompts, short partner interactions and a text-only fallback when projector/speaker routing fails. **ADAPT** into adult professional Legal/Business content only; no importing student or school information into EnglishLegalese.
