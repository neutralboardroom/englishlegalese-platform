## Material improvement, not cosmetic change
HF93 multiple-choice checks understanding of a whole two-person conversation; HF94 and HF95 add optional audio and individual-turn rehearsal. HF96 adds a distinct deeper skill: **explain the speaker's meaning in a new complete English sentence**, rather than merely echoing the turn. Each of 12 existing turns has a checked original sample paraphrase, connected by exact matching to the original speaker's displayed words. The user speaks or thinks before revealing the sample and compares the two personally. No speech/audio recording, microphone, AI inference, remote requests, personal data collection, automatic correction, fluency scoring or implied legal/financial advice. If the turn no longer matches the authored evidence, the sample fails closed rather than showing an incorrect meaning.
