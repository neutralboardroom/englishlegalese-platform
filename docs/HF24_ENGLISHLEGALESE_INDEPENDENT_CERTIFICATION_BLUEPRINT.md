# EnglishLegalese HF24 — Original Professional English Certification Design (provisional)

**Owner:** EnglishLegalese. **Issue state:** NOT ISSUING, NOT BOOKABLE. **Reference:** Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume (2020) for general language performance; EnglishLegalese must conduct original assessment validation before making any claims of equivalence.

## Independent certification tracks

1. **EnglishLegalese Legal English Certification** — Legal workplace language only, unique exam family `EL-LEGAL`, separate certificate serial and result. Not a lawyer qualification, legal advice, bar certification, TOLES qualification, or Council of Europe certificate.
2. **EnglishLegalese Business English Certification** — Business workplace language only, unique exam family `EL-BUSINESS`, separate certificate serial and result. Not a Cambridge BEC, Linguaskill Business, TOEIC, or Council of Europe certificate.

Both use a **shared format** for original issuer branding, A1–C2 as *provisional CEFR-informed level references*, timestamp, versioned syllabus/rubric, permitted accommodations, exam identity, assessor(s), and validation reference. **No combined or inferred certificate level.**

## Provisional assessment blueprint for each level, in each track

| Level | Legal English, observed live task | Business English, observed live task |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Simple legal-office introduction, term identification, supervised short note | Workplace introduction, schedule and brief written message |
| A2 | Live document-request clarification and short client-facing email | Routine meeting logistics, customer or teammate email |
| B1 | Fictional client update and factual contract summary | Team-status briefing and project-update writing |
| B2 | Unseen clause interpretation, nuanced communication and timed memo | Unseen business case, meeting negotiation and written proposal |
| C1 | Complex simulated client or counterpart negotiation with written synthesis | Executive presentation, difficult stakeholder questions and business analysis |
| C2 | Extended unfamiliar legal-language discussion and conflicting document synthesis | Unfamiliar boardroom case, dense document synthesis and live examiner challenge |

Each level uses a **separate track-specific test form**, with unseen prompts, alternate equivalent form for resits, teacher-paced live tasks, independent marking and accommodations. Shared rating domains, provisionally weighted: interactive spoken production 25%, reading and source comprehension 20%, listening and clarification 15%, writing 25%, mediation and evidence-based synthesis 15%. These are **proposed**, not validated cut scores. Do not compute or publish a CEFR-equivalence score until performance standards are set through a recognized standard-setting and validation study.

## Examination controls before activation

- Live authorized human proctor/examiner checks identity, records start/end and authorized aids, randomizes unseen tasks, monitors real-time oral follow-up and controls writing prompts. AI/outside assistance forbidden during assessment; live supervision cannot guarantee elimination of cheating.
- Evidence chain includes examiner role checks, learner consent, exam form/version, rubric version, written/oral task provenance, score moderation for close decisions, retention and privacy controls, accommodation review, appeal/resit process and separate issuer approval.
- Teachers cannot issue their own certificates or assign levels through ordinary lesson scores. A dedicated permissioned issuance workflow and tamper-evident verification service require separate development, security/privacy review and approval.
- Employer viewing requires verified issuer record plus learner-controlled sharing/consent, not raw student answers or teacher comments. No automatic employment determination.
- Do not use the Council of Europe emblem/logo, imply Council of Europe endorsement, or claim official CEFR testing. Keep independent status conspicuous on all public pages and the certificate itself.
