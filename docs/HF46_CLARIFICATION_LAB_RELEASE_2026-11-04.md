# HF46 — 24 original information-gap and polite clarification practice labs
2026-11-04. Parent HF45 6c033c295baefcbd3b72e6f6b1b6fa79a26cb17c; third cumulative material release.

**Before:** learners practiced model dialogues and follow-ups, but did not systematically notice what was missing from an ambiguous professional message before asking for verification. **After:** each lesson adds a new original ambiguous workplace statement, a three-step paraphrase → identify missing facts → ask and confirm exercise, and a hidden topic-specific carefully qualified model (missing data, clarifying question and next confirmation). There are 24 complete original scenarios. Teacher can read messages aloud once, or learner can practice independently with text. No microphone/audio recording or disclosure, no AI score or false certification.

Supports source-aware legal language and balanced business reporting; avoids treating unverified facts as legal advice. Models mediation, interaction and listening/reading comprehension recommended in Council of Europe CEFR Companion volume (2024) and Cambridge English teacher guidance (2026 live docs). Intro previews careful fictional usage. No change to approved homepage, 24 existing cards, HF44-45, pricing, membership, dashboards, accounts, scheduling, Stripe, email, production DB.
