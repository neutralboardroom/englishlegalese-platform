## Real learning improvement

HF85 adds a three-exchange **scripted branching dialogue** to the existing private writing studio, with two professional scenarios per independent track. Every exchange offers two fully authored responses. Each produces its **own** prewritten stakeholder reaction, English language-coaching explanation and an existing bilingual professional rationale. The reaction becomes context for the next exchange. All three rounds can be deliberately completed, restarted or abandoned. There is no automatic grade or AI-generated reply.

| Program | Contexts | Scripted exchanges | Alternative reactions |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Legal English | Contract clause clarification; careful negotiation language | 6 | 12 |
| Business English | Project status update; client priorities meeting | 6 | 12 |
| **Total** | **4** | **12** | **24** |

The English source is invariant across five explanation locales: `en-US`, `th-TH`, `vi-VN`, `zh-CN`, `ko-KR`. The native-language and professional explanation content remains **DRAFT_NATIVE_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED**; no automated script asserts approval by a translator or lawyer.

## Explicit learner and teacher controls

- Learner must choose and press **Send my selected reply** before seeing the reaction. The next exchange is disabled until that deliberate check.
- The next prompt includes the **previous stakeholder's reaction**; this is branching authored dialogue rather than a randomized or externally generated conversation.
- The three-round recap is assembled only after all three exchanges; the user can selectively copy their chosen lines into the existing editable writing field.
- If a writing draft already contains anything, copying it requires a **second explicit confirmation**. Editing the draft between clicks cancels that confirmation.
- Scene, locale, course, lesson stage and STOP clear the ephemeral conversation, pending overwrite confirmations, and writing draft. No localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, voice recording, microphone, network or client-data ingestion is introduced.
- Learners can try both branches, discuss why an alternative was more measured, and use the continuing private writing exercise. Teacher Roger's actual voice/avatar/AI is **not connected**. Nothing constitutes legal advice, teacher assessment, certification or a completed paid lesson.

## Practical lesson use

For a teacher-led 50–60 minute adult lesson: open with five minutes of important vocabulary, model two respectful choices, allow learners to run three exchanges independently or in pairs (10–15 minutes), ask them to compare the stakeholder consequences (10 minutes), have learners rewrite their selected replies (10–15 minutes), and finish with a personal professional goal and next-session preview. All student prompts should use invented facts. The Legal English track should never imply that correct grammar guarantees a particular legal effect.
