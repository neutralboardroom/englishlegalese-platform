# EnglishLegalese — permanent dashboard and certification continuation contract
**Owner decisions consolidated: October 29, 2026. Applies to HF24 / v1.85.10 and every subsequent website version until explicitly superseded by the owner.**

## Binding scope and release discipline
This is a website build continuation specification, not a request to generate another Universal Builder. Each successor builds from the **true latest qualified/live website source**, preserves all previous usable features and locked desktop hero/logo, keeps the domain/course structure, runs inherited and new regression tests, deploys only once both independently qualified exact-head CI checks pass, and verifies the resulting Render commit and both public domain variants. Never claim a pending branch is live or that a staged UI feature is already provisioned in production. No production data reset, destructive migration, invented course booking, invented score, automatically dispatched marketing or unverified payment. Necessary transactional email may be enabled only in approved event-driven, verified, rate-limited flows; no unsolicited marketing. Keep separated roles and private student submissions.

## Five role and product workstreams — all mandatory carry-forward
### Student dashboard
- Verified login, account email safety, secure signout and all-device session revocation; transactional-only verification/reset/essential class notifications.
- Save, pause and resume every independent self-study activity on every supported device after login; clear unauthenticated limitations.
- Per-lesson saved work index with search, filtering, resume links, last-updated timestamps, submission/revision state, exact server pagination, local device export/import, cloud backup and recovery, and never overwrite existing student drafts without explicit consent.
- Genuine feedback history: distinguish latest vs earlier entries, reviewed/awaiting/unassigned counts limited to actual returned submissions, reviewed-only filter, explicit download, no fabricated teacher assignment, safe teacher text, and stale-session suppression.
- Training and certification track, separately: evidence-backed course progress (not merely self-practice), assessment eligibility, consent-driven employer progress visibility, subject-specific assessment results and authenticity checks.
- Preserve student privacy: employers see **only** verified, consented, explicitly published report fields, never drafts, private uploads, AI conversations, raw grading comments, private teacher notes, financial information or other employers' data.

### Teacher dashboard
- Verified teacher-only login and safe revocation, only teacher-assigned submissions, filters/search/pagination with accurate bounds, refresh and status.
- Assigned lesson and submission reader; actual student-owned work available only for authorized instruction, bounded written feedback, draft-saving if safe, one deliberate submit, review status and revision history; no duplicate review writes.
- Next versions: teacher calendar with real confirmed classes, roster and attendance capture, classroom links when provider verified, homework scheduling/marking, rubrics, reassessment, report publication approval, and certification testing.
- Separate supervised Legal English and Business English original question banks at each A1–C2 level; unseen prompts, identity and permitted-tools checks, teacher observed live oral and timed written evidence, rubric scoring, second assessor for borderline cases, conflict checks, appeal/resit trail. Never generate credentials from unsupervised AI/self-study or course attendance alone.

### Employer dashboard
- Secure **employer account** associated with verified, active organization-admin membership. Enforce account role, active organization membership, current employee/student membership, and reporting consent on every server request, never only in the UI.
- Live employer-specific progress/results: learner names only when permitted; published course/attendance/completion/results/verified certificate data, level, milestones, timestamps, scope, explanatory no-data states, refresh and explicit secure CSV/PDF export, filters and multi-company selector. A null metric means “Not reported”, never a zero or invented completion.
- Employer chooses current eligible employees, training track (Legal, Business, Investor or combined), package/seat quantity, time preferences; server validates roster and price, produces idempotent recorded **pending** requests with history/status, notifications only on verified real events. Do not claim enrollment or class schedule confirmed before staff acts.
- Add explicit employee invitations/verification and consents, staff approval and seat assignments; avoid treating email address alone as an authorized company employee.
- Payments: company quotes, volume tiers, billing and invoice review, real Stripe Checkout only with server-side product/price controls, signed webhook settlement, duplicate protection, refunds/failed-payment handling, receipts, and exact reconciliation to confirmed seats. Disable live payment or mark unready until genuinely verified. Never use a mock checkout or imply payment occurred.
- Track completed/eligible professional English credentials separately, with share/verification consent. Do not imply CEFR, TOLES, Cambridge or government endorsement.
- Companies cannot impersonate employees, see personal drafts/teacher notes, or inspect other companies.

### Owner/admin dashboard
- Real owner and admin role-gated operations, with distinction among owner/admin/staff permissions, no public owner interface or self-service privilege escalation.
- Read-only aggregate verified counts first (accounts by role, active organizations, classes and statuses, teacher review backlog, published reports) plus task/action queues and incomplete-readiness signals.
- Next versions: verified enrollment-inquiry review, staffing/teacher approvals, schedule and seat confirmation, authorization/invitation and employer consent administration, manual report publishing/revocation, certification assessor controls and issue/revoke audit, successful payments/refunds/margin/payout status and reconciliation, exports, error logs, data integrity/backup/restore and integration health.
- Never show invented financial figures or expose secrets, student work or unredacted personal data in a public widget. Scope sensitive reads/writes, audit all privileged changes, require deliberate confirmation.

### Independent EnglishLegalese certification offerings
- Advertise **EnglishLegalese Legal English Certification** and **EnglishLegalese Business English Certification** as separate EnglishLegalese-owned, CEFR-informed specialist offerings, with matching professional presentation but distinct question banks, assessment evidence, scores, level decisions, issuing IDs and verification pages.
- Keep a concise employer-value mention inside homepage **main hero paragraph**, a Certifications menu item, comprehensive overview page, and separate full Legal English and Business English certification pages; linked from each subject course page and employer views. Wording can be confident about the program offering but must never falsely claim a certificate was already awarded, a candidate can book a currently unavailable assessment, or external accreditation exists.
- Track A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2 using original skills-based rubrics informed by CEFR. CEFR is a general proficiency framework **not** an issuing/accreditation body. Do not claim CEFR validation/equivalence without credible formal studies, assessor calibration and external review. Do not suggest EnglishLegalese grants a law license, professional qualification, official TOLES, Cambridge or TOEIC award.
- Exams must be **actually supervised in live human-teacher sessions** with identity verification, unseen materials, secure written/oral evidence, anti-AI rules, second review on borderline cases, documented decisions and appeals, and issuer-grade tamper-resistant verifiable unique certificates. Anti-cheating measures mitigate but cannot guarantee zero misuse.
- Before launch implement issuance records (subject, exact level, score bands, assessor, date, unique serial, revocation state), restricted signing, candidate approval/consent, verification page and tests that fail closed. No fabricated award or fictitious serial.
