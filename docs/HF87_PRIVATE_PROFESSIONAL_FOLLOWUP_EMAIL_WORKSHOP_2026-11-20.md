## New substantive English teaching practice

Twelve scenario-specific follow-up email cases (three exchanges for each of four fictional-adult situations) are offered inside both existing Legal English and Business English tracks: clause clarification, negotiating deadlines, project updates, and client meetings.

Each case has a specific authored English subject, courteous context paragraph, action request and model sign-off. Learners draft independent editable English text in five clearly labeled fields: subject, greeting, context, requested action, closing. This is not a one-click template generator: the learner must write a meaningful subject, context and request before preview or model comparison. The fully formatted local-only draft can be explicitly inserted into the original private writing field, with a second confirmation before replacing existing text. Editing cancels previous confirmation; overlong drafts (>1200 characters) are rejected rather than truncated. An explicit Clear control, scenario changes, language/track changes and STOP erase ephemeral content.

The English remains English-first; supporting instructions and scenario rationale are provided in `en-US`, `th-TH`, `vi-VN`, `zh-CN`, and `ko-KR` draft form. All non-English content requires fluent native-speaking and legal/business professional review before publication. Properly authored English model answers are only revealed after the learner has written the three required email parts AND explicitly requests comparison. Different professional wording may be appropriate. No AI scores the response.

The feature does not contact or send messages to clients, use the network, generate personalized legal advice, register real pupils, attach account data, store inputs, connect an AI model/voice/avatar, or activate billing, emails or payments. Classroom, Online, CloneFusion and ControlCenter source and releases remain strictly READ-ONLY.
