# EnglishLegalese HF82 - Private Response Writing Studio (development only)

**Workstream:** TR-EnglishLegalese ONLY. Not TR-Classroom, TR-Online, TR-CloneFusion or TR-ControlCenter. **Release:** HF82 / cumulative Universal Builder V4.2. **Status:** draft / not live / no real avatar or generative AI.

## What this actually improves

HF82 allows fictional adult Legal and Business English learners to **author their own professional reply**, rather than merely selecting a canned two-option answer. The original HF81 twelve independent dialogue turns across four scenarios are reused verbatim, yielding full English prompts, a native-language explanation and precise English model replies. A new isolated `details` writing studio is embedded under the HF81 Conversation Lab in step four of the five-stage lesson. The new pedagogical loop is:

1. Read the English fictional question; optionally use bilingual instructions in **English, Thai, Vietnamese, Simplified Mandarin or Korean**.
2. Write a complete **English** response in the locally ephemeral textarea (maximum 1200 characters; live word count). Do not enter real names, client matters, confidential data or other personal information.
3. Self-review **meaning/accuracy**, **professional tone** and **specific next action, limitation or deadline**. Checkboxes are learner reflection, *not* an AI score or teacher assessment.
4. Deliberately click **Compare with model** to see English model, full English rationale, native-language explanation and review guidance. A different reasonable answer may also be correct; no automatic semantic assessment occurs.
5. Editing a draft hides a previously revealed model until deliberately reopened. Clearing, changing turn, changing scenario, navigating, language/course change, STOP or restart destroys the draft and self-check state. No network, microphone, data storage, account, provider call, or grade is involved.
