24 Legal English sessions: subject by subject

All Legal English topics have independent four-part workbooks as well as lesson objectives, terminology, fictional practice, homework and teacher briefs. The teacher-led complete pathway uses one live session per topic, not four automatically taught meetings per topic.

24 Business and Investor English sessions: subject by subject

Sixteen general Business English topics, followed by eight Wall Street and Investor English topics. Professional financial-language instruction is not investment advice.

How a full-pathway lesson works

Each subject has a 50-minute instructor plan: 5 minutes of review and goals; 10 minutes for vocabulary and careful model language; 15 minutes applying the fictional subject-specific case; 15 minutes for learner speaking and writing; 5 minutes for individualized feedback and homework. Independent learning materials support further practice. Teaching staff and all sessions must be approved before delivery.
