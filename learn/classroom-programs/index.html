16-session Legal English and Business English classroom plans

Explore the independent 16 × 50-minute programs. Each session includes a language objective, vocabulary, staged practice, fictional conversation exercise, model response, homework and teacher evaluation prompts. The Wall Street track remains an optional focus within Business English.

How a 50-minute class works

Five-minute check-in, 10-minute source and vocabulary briefing, 20-minute roleplay and comprehension checks, 10-minute independent drafting and feedback, and five-minute homework assignment and reflection. Teachers adapt timing for access needs and proficiency.

All cases are fictional. These are detailed instructional guides, not a guaranteed class placement or personalized legal or investment advice. Instructor assignment and meeting links are confirmed privately after enrollment review.

Legal English · 16 independent sessions

Mondays at 7:00 p.m. Vietnam time starting November 2, 2026; each group class lasts 50 minutes. Visit Legal English.

Business English · 16 independent sessions

Wednesdays at 7:00 p.m. Vietnam time starting November 4, 2026. Financial-language specializations can be substituted where a suitable teacher and learner preference are confirmed. Visit Business English.

Enrollment and instructor preparation

Each session includes a student deliverable, a teacher roleplay prompt and a four-point language feedback check. Teachers should assign exercises only after reviewing suitability, learning level and availability.
