For individual professionals and employers

Practicing lawyers and law firms

Explain clauses, ask precise client questions, manage legal-English calls, present qualified advice, negotiate and accurately summarize provisional decisions—using fictional teaching scenarios.

In-house legal and compliance teams

Discuss contract review, approvals, obligations, purchase terms, service levels, due diligence and discussions with overseas colleagues. Ask about private lessons or a controlled employer training plan.

Business and project professionals

Use English for investor, supplier, energy and infrastructure meetings, presentations, commercial correspondence, procurement negotiations and cross-border teams.
