Business English · 16-session classroom teaching outline

Every session has a language objective, vocabulary with definitions, a practical activity, homework and teacher feedback guidance. The 50-minute sequence is suggested and can be adapted by the assigned teacher. These materials are original educational outlines rather than recorded lessons or confirmed places.

Week 1 · Workplace communication baseline

Learning objective: Introduce a role and a credible work objective

50-minute outline: 5 minutes review; 10 minutes modeling; 10 minutes vocabulary; 15 minutes interactive fictional practice; 10 minutes feedback and preparation for the next class.

Vocabulary and definitions

  • role: job function
  • deliverable: expected result
  • stakeholder: affected participant
  • priority: relative importance

Interactive practice

Introduce a fictional cross-border assignment and invite questions

Homework

Write a short work introduction with objectives and constraints

Teacher materials and assessment

Baseline confidence, pronunciation and clarity

Week 2 · Business email

Learning objective: Make requests, timelines and responses clear and polite

50-minute outline: 5 minutes review; 10 minutes modeling; 10 minutes vocabulary; 15 minutes interactive fictional practice; 10 minutes feedback and preparation for the next class.

Vocabulary and definitions

  • subject: email topic
  • request: action sought
  • deadline: due date
  • follow-up: subsequent message

Interactive practice

Ask a fictional colleague for a late report

Homework

Draft two concise email versions for different audiences

Teacher materials and assessment

Coach request clarity and realistic commitments
