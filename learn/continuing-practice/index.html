Legal English — six continuing lessons

Legal English · lesson 1

Client intake: verified facts

Fictional situation: A client reports that equipment arrived eight days late; the signed delivery schedule has not yet been checked.

Professional vocabulary: reported claim; verification; chronology

Read an original model response
The client reports a late delivery. We have not verified the contractual deadline because the signed schedule is not yet available. We will compare the schedule with the delivery record before commenting on any contractual implications.

Your writing assignment: Write three sentences: one attributed claim, one unverified fact and a next step.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

The second statement attributes the allegation and preserves the evidence boundary.

Teacher-led sessions require separate verified booking.

Legal English · lesson 2

Contract notices and deadlines

Fictional situation: A fictional agreement says written notice must be delivered to a particular address within ten business days after a triggering event.

Professional vocabulary: designated address; triggering event; mandatory notice

Read an original model response
The agreement requires written notice to the specified address within ten business days. Please confirm the triggering date, the method of delivery and the current notice details before a final deadline is calculated.

Your writing assignment: Replace undefinedSend it soonundefined with a neutral instruction identifying the requirement and missing inputs.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

A deadline cannot be calculated reliably until the applicable facts are confirmed.

Legal English · lesson 3

Redlining: modal verbs and cost scope

Fictional situation: A counterparty revises undefinedshall reimburse all costsundefined to undefinedmay reimburse reasonable costsundefined.

Professional vocabulary: shall; may; reasonable

Read an original model response
The revision changes mandatory language into discretionary language and adds a limitation relating to reasonable costs. Before accepting the edit, we should clarify whether both changes are intended and assess their commercial consequences.

Your writing assignment: Write an email explaining the two language changes in no more than 100 words.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

The modal verb and qualifying adjective both materially affect meaning.

Legal English · lesson 4

Witness interviews: open questions

Fictional situation: A witness may have observed a manager signing a page but cannot remember which document was involved.

Professional vocabulary: open-ended; leading; recollection

Read an original model response
Please describe what you remember about the documents discussed at the meeting. Did you see anyone sign anything? What do you remember about the page? It is fine to say that you are unsure.

Your writing assignment: Convert undefinedYou saw the manager sign the contract, didnundefinedt you?undefined into two open questions.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

Open questions permit the witness to state their own recollection.

Legal English · lesson 5

Negotiation: conditions and tentative terms

Fictional situation: Two companies are negotiating a revised payment schedule but still need approvals.

Professional vocabulary: subject to; in principle; outstanding

Read an original model response
We are prepared to discuss a revised schedule, subject to agreement on the outstanding terms and necessary approvals. This outline is a proposal for discussion, not a record of a completed settlement.

Your writing assignment: Draft a conditional counterproposal with one open point and one next step.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

A conditional proposal must not be presented as a concluded agreement.

Cross-border drafting: clarify meaning

Fictional situation: Lawyers in different jurisdictions use a specialized term differently in a fictional agreement.

Professional vocabulary: governing law; term of art; clarification

Read an original model response
Could you clarify the intended meaning of undefinedundertakingundefined here and the governing-law context assumed by the draft? Aligning the intended obligation would help our teams review the clause consistently.

Your writing assignment: Write a neutral request for clarification without claiming one universal definition.

Business and Investor English — six continuing lessons

Business English · lesson 1

Quarterly reporting: balanced trends

Fictional situation: A divisionundefineds revenue has risen but its operating margin has fallen. The cause is not confirmed.

Professional vocabulary: revenue; margin; driver

Read an original model response
Quarterly revenue increased compared with the prior period, while operating margin declined. The finance team is reviewing the cost and product mix figures before identifying the main drivers of the movement.

Your writing assignment: Draft an executive summary covering both metrics and one uncertainty.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

Balanced reporting avoids inventing the cause of an unexplained trend.

Business English · lesson 2

Project handovers: clear ownership

Fictional situation: Sales is transferring an implementation project to operations; a key specification has not arrived.

Professional vocabulary: owner; dependency; escalation

Read an original model response
The operations lead will propose a testing schedule by Tuesday. Finalizing the checklist depends on receiving the clientundefineds approved specifications. We will record the owner and next review date for each outstanding action.

Your writing assignment: Write a handover note assigning two actions, owners and deadlines.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

Dependencies and owners make a handover actionable.

Business English · lesson 3

Supplier negotiation: constructive trade-offs

Fictional situation: A supplier can expedite only part of an order and has not confirmed the extra charge.

Professional vocabulary: trade-off; expedite; feasible

Read an original model response
If full delivery by our target date is not feasible, could you prioritize the components needed for the initial production run? Please confirm the earliest delivery and any extra cost before we decide.

Your writing assignment: Draft a counterproposal asking about priority goods, timing and price.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

A good counterproposal asks for the information needed to compare alternatives.

Business English · lesson 4

Investor relations: guidance and assumptions

Fictional situation: An analyst asks whether the companyundefineds next-quarter forecast is guaranteed.

Professional vocabulary: guidance; assumption; forecast

Read an original model response
Our current guidance reflects the assumptions in the presentation. Actual results may differ as market conditions and demand change. We will provide updates through our usual reporting process when appropriate.

Teacher extension

Business English · lesson 5

Performance reviews: observable feedback

Fictional situation: A colleague prepared accurate reports but missed two agreed internal handover deadlines.

Professional vocabulary: observation; impact; action plan

Read an original model response
Your customer reports were accurate and well organized. Two handover summaries arrived after the agreed deadlines, delaying review. Letundefineds establish an earlier progress checkpoint for the next cycle.

Your writing assignment: Write three feedback sentences: strength, observable concern and next step.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

Describing actions and impact is more useful than guessing motivation.

Business English · lesson 6

Board presentations: uncertainty and mitigation

Fictional situation: A software rollout has an unresolved data-integration risk and a fallback process under test.

Professional vocabulary: exposure; mitigation; contingency

Read an original model response
The main delivery risk is the unresolved integration. We are testing a fallback workflow and will report results before recommending a launch date. The current schedule remains provisional.

Your writing assignment: Draft a 45-second board update showing the risk, mitigation and decision checkpoint.

Choose the better professional wording

Teacher extension

A professional update makes uncertainty and the decision process explicit.
