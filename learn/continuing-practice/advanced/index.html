Legal English · new lesson 7 · B2–C1

Research summary: sources versus conclusions

Fictional workplace scenario: A fictional legal team receives a short research note that cites two court decisions but has not checked whether either is controlling in the relevant jurisdiction. A client wants an immediate yes-or-no answer.

Specialist vocabulary: authority; jurisdiction; qualification

Read the model professional response
The two decisions identified in the initial search discuss similar contractual language, but we have not yet checked their current status or applicability to the relevant jurisdiction. Our preliminary summary therefore identifies a potential argument rather than a settled answer. We recommend verifying the decisions and reviewing the governing-law clause before advising on the likely outcome.

Writing assignment: Draft a 90-word research update distinguishing what the sources say, what remains unverified, and one next research task.

Choose the clearest professional statement

Unsupervised practice feedback only; not an exam or teacher assessment.

Optional instructor extension

Have students brief a partner on one fictional court extract and identify any unverified assumption. Classroom availability and scheduling are confirmed separately.

Legal English · new lesson 8 · B2–C1

Disclosure request: precise scope and privacy

Fictional workplace scenario: In a fictional dispute, a team needs emails about a supply interruption, but one draft request says undefinedsend everything about the companyundefined and the company is concerned about unrelated personal records.

Specialist vocabulary: relevance; scope; privileged information

Read the model professional response
Could you identify correspondence concerning the supply interruption during the specified three-month period, including the agreed project teams and relevant contract references? Please preserve potentially responsive records and flag documents that may raise confidentiality or privilege issues for appropriate review before sharing. We should agree the search scope and applicable obligations with counsel.

Writing assignment: Rewrite the overly broad request into a targeted email specifying time, topic, document type, and appropriate review.

Legal English · new lesson 9 · B2–C2

Mediation statement: interests without admissions

Fictional workplace scenario: Two fictional suppliers dispute the cause of a canceled shipment. Both want to explore solutions in mediation but disagree about what happened, and no settlement has been signed.

Specialist vocabulary: position; interest; without admission

Read the model professional response
Our client disputes the account of the cancellation but recognizes the commercial costs associated with prolonged disagreement. The immediate priorities are continuity of supply, a reliable delivery schedule and a proportionate process for resolving the outstanding invoice. We are willing to explore settlement options without characterizing this discussion as an admission of responsibility.

Writing assignment: Write a concise opening that distinguishes the disputed factual position from two shared commercial interests.

Choose the clearest professional statement

Unsupervised practice feedback only; not an exam or teacher assessment.

Optional instructor extension

Simulate a mediator asking each side for its interests and a possible next step, not legal conclusions. Classroom availability and scheduling are confirmed separately.

Legal English · new lesson 10 · B1–C1

Matter management: deadline and dependency updates

Fictional workplace scenario: A legal team has a draft contract to review by Thursday, but a client has not delivered a technical schedule. The manager needs a status update that does not promise completion regardless of missing information.

Specialist vocabulary: dependency; milestone; escalation

Read the model professional response
We have completed the first-pass review of the commercial terms. Finalizing the service-level section depends on receiving the technical schedule requested from the client. If it arrives by Tuesday, we expect to circulate a consolidated draft on Thursday; otherwise we will update the timeline and identify which provisions remain open.

Writing assignment: Compose a progress note with a finished item, a missing input, a conditional date and an escalation step.

Unsupervised practice feedback only; not an exam or teacher assessment.

Optional instructor extension

Have partners conduct a five-minute matter update using completed, blocked, owner and next review. Classroom availability and scheduling are confirmed separately.

Legal English · new lesson 12 · B1–C1

Client update: options, risks and decision owner

Fictional workplace scenario: A fictional client is deciding whether to accept a revised contract delivery schedule. The legal adviser has identified two options, but the business impact and counterparty acceptance are not yet confirmed.

Specialist vocabulary: option; consequence; recommendation

Read the model professional response
There are two practical paths for discussion: request a revised delivery commitment or explore a staged handover. Each would require the counterpartyundefineds agreement, and the operational effect should be reviewed by your project team. We can help clarify the language and document your preferred approach once you decide which commercial priority matters most.

Writing assignment: Draft an 80-word client email explaining both options, what remains unknown and who must decide.

Business English · new lesson 7 · B2–C2

Investor briefing: cash flow and uncertainty

Fictional workplace scenario: A fictional growth company announces higher sales but negative operating cash flow, while investors ask whether the cash situation will improve next quarter.

Specialist vocabulary: cash flow; runway; assumption

Read the model professional response
Sales increased during the period, but operating cash flow remained negative. Management is reviewing the drivers of working-capital movement and spending. Our current liquidity planning reflects assumptions about collections and operating costs; we cannot guarantee the timing or size of future cash-flow improvement.

Writing assignment: Prepare a 70-word investor response balancing growth, liquidity risk and an appropriate future-looking qualification.

Unsupervised practice feedback only; not an exam or teacher assessment.

Optional instructor extension

Simulate an analyst call and ask participants to distinguish reported results from forecasts. Classroom availability and scheduling are confirmed separately.

Business English · new lesson 8 · B1–C1

Incident communications: facts before blame

Fictional workplace scenario: A fictional organization experiences a customer login outage. The support team knows the service is disrupted but has not confirmed the root cause or an exact restoration time.

Specialist vocabulary: incident; investigation; update cadence

Read the model professional response
We are investigating an issue affecting some account logins. Our technical team is working to restore normal access, and we will provide the next status update at 15:00 local time. We do not yet have a confirmed root cause or reliable restoration estimate, and we will share further details once verified.

Writing assignment: Draft a 60-word status message with confirmed impact, action, next update and no invented restoration guarantee.

Unsupervised practice feedback only; not an exam or teacher assessment.

Optional instructor extension

Business English · new lesson 9 · B2–C1

Service negotiations: measurable promises

Fictional workplace scenario: A buyer asks a provider to promise undefinedexcellent support at all timesundefined, while neither side has agreed on response times, coverage hours or escalation procedures.

Specialist vocabulary: service level; metric; escalation

Read the model professional response
We are open to defining a service commitment, but the wording should state the covered hours, response-time measurement and escalation process. For example, we could discuss an initial response target for priority-one tickets during agreed coverage periods. We should confirm service capacity and remedies before adopting the final terms.

Writing assignment: Replace undefinedexcellent supportundefined with two measurable parameters and one question about operational capacity.

Feedback after a project: evidence and improvement

Fictional workplace scenario: After a fictional product launch, a project lead wants to recognize the team while addressing missed review checkpoints without blaming a particular colleague.

Specialist vocabulary: retrospective; observation; action item

Business English · new lesson 11 · B2–C2

Executive decision memo: compare trade-offs

Fictional workplace scenario: A fictional retailer can either expand to a new region this quarter or improve its existing delivery network. Forecasts for both options contain uncertainties.

Specialist vocabulary: trade-off; scenario; recommendation

Read the model professional response
Option A could accelerate market entry but would require additional hiring and initial distribution investment. Option B focuses resources on improving delivery reliability in existing locations. Both cases depend on assumptions about demand and execution. I recommend comparing downside scenarios and agreeing on decision criteria before selecting the preferred option.
