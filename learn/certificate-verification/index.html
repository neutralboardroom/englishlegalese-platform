Verify an EnglishLegalese certificate

Use the certificate's unique serial number and confidential verification code to confirm whether an EnglishLegalese Legal English or Business English credential is currently valid.

The verification process shares only the credential track, specialist level, issue date and validity status. It does not reveal identity documents, exam answers or private teacher feedback. Candidate consent is required for public verification.

Availability: Credential verification will work for authentic EnglishLegalese certificates once the secure issuance system is operational. No certificates are presently represented as issued by this page.

A verified EnglishLegalese specialist-language result is not an official Council of Europe CEFR certificate, a TOLES examination result, or an academic or professional license.
