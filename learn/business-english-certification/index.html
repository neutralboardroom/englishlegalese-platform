EnglishLegalese Business English Certification

This is a separate, EnglishLegalese-branded credential for meetings, business email, presentations, management, workplace negotiation and financial or investor communications. It has its own assessments, results and certificate records. Scores in the other specialist track do not transfer or automatically establish a level.

How a candidate would earn a certificate

  1. Choose this specialist track and request an assessment time; ordinary self-study scores do not qualify.
  2. Complete identity verification and live human-proctored speaking, listening, reading, written-production and interaction tasks using unseen, original prompts.
  3. Follow the examiner's authorized-tool rules. AI assistance, outside coaching and unapproved materials are prohibited during examination tasks; supervision reduces but cannot eliminate misconduct.
  4. Have two trained assessors moderate borderline results using recorded scoring rubrics, with a documented appeal and resit process.
  5. Only after the entire examination, scoring, security and issuer-verification system is approved would EnglishLegalese issue a uniquely numbered, date-stamped credential naming the exact specialist track and level.

Status: No live certification exam booking, public credential verification or credential issuance is enabled yet. Training and classroom inquiries are available separately.

International reference: CEFR-informed, not CEFR-issued

EnglishLegalese proposes its own specialist A1–C2 level descriptions inspired by the Council of Europe's Common European Framework of Reference for Languages and its 2020 Companion volume. The CEFR provides general language-proficiency descriptors, not an externally awarded Legal English or Business English credential. A claimed CEFR level-equivalence would require careful standard-setting, validation, assessor training, empirical evidence and external review.

Official sources: Council of Europe CEFR framework and CEFR can-do descriptors.

Independence: An EnglishLegalese Business English certificate would be issued solely by EnglishLegalese, not by the Council of Europe, Cambridge, TOEIC, TOLES or any professional licensing body. It would assess workplace language, not legal knowledge, authorization to practice law or professional competence beyond language.
