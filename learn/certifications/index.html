EnglishLegalese Professional English Certifications

EnglishLegalese offers two separate, original professional English certification pathways based on demonstrated communication skills, with live, teacher-supervised testing and levels informed by international CEFR standards.

Our two independent credentials

EnglishLegalese Legal English Certification

A1–C2 specialist levels for legal workplace communication, contracts and client interactions.

Explore Legal English certification

EnglishLegalese Business English Certification

Proposed A1–C2 specialist levels for workplace meetings, presentations, business writing and investor communication.

Explore Business English certification

These credential programs use matching professional formats, independently administered subject-specific tests and separate level results. Neither one certifies the other.

What certification would mean

Each certificate documents the candidate's verified specialist-English performance on original, proctored assessments—not attendance alone, AI practice, or completion of a course. Each credential identifies its track, assessment date, awarded level, issuer and verification identifier when assessment and issuer verification are activated.

Levels are CEFR-informed references, not CEFR-issued certificates. EnglishLegalese has not established external accreditation or a validated CEFR-equivalence study. We will not issue exams or certificates until live supervision, identity confirmation, rubrics, quality assurance, appeals and verification controls are ready.

For employers

Current and prospective employers can use a candidate's independently verified, job-relevant Legal English or Business English result as one signal in evaluating language needs—not as a hiring decision made automatically. A learner's confidential practice answers and teacher notes are not part of employer reports.

Read the official CEFR framework for international context.
