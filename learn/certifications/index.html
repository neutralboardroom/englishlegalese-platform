These credentials would have matching professional formats, independently administered subject-specific tests and separate level results. Neither one certifies the other.

What certification would mean

A certificate would document the candidate's observed specialist-English performance on original, proctored assessments—not attendance alone, AI practice, or completion of a course. Each credential would identify its track, assessment date, awarded level, issuer and verification identifier after the validation and issuance system is operational.

Levels are CEFR-informed references, not CEFR-issued certificates. EnglishLegalese has not established external accreditation or a validated CEFR-equivalence study. We will not issue exams or certificates until live supervision, identity confirmation, rubrics, quality assurance, appeals and verification controls are ready.

For employers

Employers could use a candidate's independently verified, job-relevant Legal English or Business English result as one signal in evaluating language needs—not as a hiring decision made automatically. A learner's confidential practice answers and teacher notes are not part of employer reports.
