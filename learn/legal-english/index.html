Legal English · 16-session classroom teaching outline

Every session has a language objective, vocabulary with definitions, a practical activity, homework and teacher feedback guidance. The 50-minute sequence is suggested and can be adapted by the assigned teacher. These materials are original educational outlines rather than recorded lessons or confirmed places.

Week 1 · Introductions and professional scope

Learning objective: Introduce a legal role accurately and ask clear questions

50-minute outline: 5 minutes review; 10 minutes modeling; 10 minutes vocabulary; 15 minutes interactive fictional practice; 10 minutes feedback and preparation for the next class.

Vocabulary and definitions

  • role: job function
  • scope: range of work
  • matter: legal project
  • clarify: make precise

Interactive practice

Introduce yourself to a fictional client and ask two open questions

Homework

Write a 90-second introduction and four clarification questions

Teacher materials and assessment

Model accurate role boundaries and neutral listening

Week 2 · Client interviews

Learning objective: Gather facts and distinguish known dates from uncertainty

50-minute outline: 5 minutes review; 10 minutes modeling; 10 minutes vocabulary; 15 minutes interactive fictional practice; 10 minutes feedback and preparation for the next class.

Vocabulary and definitions

  • intake: initial interview
  • timeline: event sequence
  • source: origin of information
  • follow-up: later inquiry

Interactive practice

Interview a fictional client with contradictory dates

Homework

Create a fact-based intake summary and list three gaps

Teacher materials and assessment

Check open questions, source attribution and no invented facts

Homework
