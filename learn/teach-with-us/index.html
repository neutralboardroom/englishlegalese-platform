Teaching opportunities and recruitment preparation

Teach Legal English or Business English with EnglishLegalese

We are planning a team of qualified teachers across Legal English, general Business English, and Wall Street & Investor English. Courses should not depend on one instructor. Explore expected qualifications, teaching standards, and the intended recruitment process.

Qualified teachers may apply now for Legal English, Business English and Wall Street & Investor English roles. Instructor selection includes qualification screening and teaching demonstration.

Review our 16-session Legal and Business English teaching plans →

Teaching tracks

Legal English

Contract reading, client-facing English, legal writing, speaking, professional ethics of language education and fictional case studies.

Business English

Meetings, professional email, sales, leadership, marketing, cross-cultural communication, presentations and global workplace vocabulary.

Wall Street & Investor English

Earnings calls, investor communications, markets, finance reporting, asset-management vocabulary and careful financial-language explanations. Finance-industry knowledge is preferred.

Teacher qualification and screening standards

  1. Strong English fluency and demonstrated subject-appropriate communication skills
  2. Verifiable relevant teaching experience and/or a recognized teaching qualification; specialist legal or finance experience reviewed separately
  3. A practical live teaching demonstration using the platform's fictional lesson material
  4. Responsible handling of student privacy, accessibility, copyright and educational boundaries
  5. Dependable scheduling, clear feedback practices, and appropriate safeguarding and professional conduct
  6. Verification of identity, qualifications, work authorization and any legally required checks before an offer

Legal-English teachers should understand the boundary between teaching language and dispensing legal advice. Financial-language teachers must avoid recommendations, personalized investing advice and implied financial outcomes.

How the multi-teacher classroom model would work

A program lead maintains a consistent syllabus and materials. Teachers may specialize by course or student level, with handover notes and standardized rubrics. Learners receive clearly identified assigned instructors once staffing and scheduling are confirmed. Instructor names and secure class access are confirmed with each enrollment; unverified teacher assignments are never advertised.

The extended Legal English and extended Business English programs each contain sixteen 50-minute classes. Each remains a separate curriculum and enrollment choice.

Classroom preparation and quality

Teacher packets include four-session lesson outlines, vocabulary, source-aware response practice, homework, evaluation rubrics and classroom feedback guidance. For specialist finance-English topics, teachers receive original fictional data and model dialogue—no live client or investment records.

Before launch, operations must establish written instructor compensation, class-size economics, contracts, scheduling, substitution arrangements, cancellation/refund rules, and course ownership.
