How ongoing memberships will work

When subscriptions become available, your chosen individual or organization membership will continue automatically for successive monthly or annual billing periods until canceled. There is no preset final membership date.

Cancel any time before the next renewal. A confirmed cancellation stops the next and future automatic renewals. You retain the access included in your already-paid period until that period ends; cancellation does not cut it short. Access ends when the paid term ends unless a new membership is purchased.

When billing launches, your account dashboard will display your plan, exact next billing date, renewal terms, cancellation control and confirmation. Cancellation must be recorded before the next renewal is processed. Refund rights, local laws, invoicing and annual terms will be explained before payment.

This describes our proposed future recurring-membership policy. No paid subscription, renewal or cancellation service is currently active.
