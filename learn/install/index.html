Optional phone app

Take EnglishLegalese with you.

You can install an app-like shortcut to our real Legal English and Business English website on supported phones, tablets and computers. Installation is your choice. You can also keep using the website normally.

Android and supported browsers

On Chrome and other supporting browsers, choose Install EnglishLegalese when your browser offers it. It opens in its own app window.

Checking whether your browser offers installation…

If no install button appears, open the browser menu and look for Install app or Add to Home screen. Browser support varies.

iPhone and iPad

In Safari, open this page, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, and confirm. Your iPhone or iPad will show an EnglishLegalese icon.

iOS usually uses the Safari menu rather than an on-page installation prompt. No App Store account is needed for this browser-based experience.

What the installed app does

It launches EnglishLegalese full-screen or in a standalone window when supported. You can browse courses, request classes and access your usual account while connected to the internet.

Offline: only a generic connection message is made available. The app does not cache private lessons, documents, account details, classroom links, AI replies or payments for offline use.

Updates to site content are delivered through the normal website and browser mechanisms. This is not a separately published native Android APK or Apple App Store application.

To remove the shortcut, use your phone's usual home-screen uninstall/remove controls. Your EnglishLegalese account and learning records are not deleted by removing the icon.
